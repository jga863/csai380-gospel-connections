# Week 2: My Personal Record-Keeping Plan

## Nephite Records

The brass plates gave Nephi’s family the commandments and history they needed. In 1 Nephi 4:13–17, the Lord tells Nephi that the records can help a whole people keep their faith. In 2 Nephi 28:2, Nephi says these written words will be valuable in the future. The records helped the Nephites remember God, their family, and their faith in Jesus Christ.

Because these records were saved, I can read the Book of Mormon today. Joseph Smith brought it forth, and its teachings help me learn about Jesus Christ and God’s promises. The stories also show me how people can keep their faith during hard times.

## Creating Your Goal

**My five-year goal:** By November 2031, I will finish college and prepare for a job in technology. I will build my skills through classwork and projects.

### Discover what I need to work on

I need to manage my time, finish my assignments, and practice my technology skills. I also need to learn which kind of technology work interests me most.

### Plan how I will do it

- At the start of each semester, I will write down my deadlines.
- Each week, I will choose three times to study.
- I will break large assignments into smaller tasks.
- Each semester, I will finish or improve one project and save it in a portfolio.
- If I need help with my school plan, I will talk with an adviser.

### Act on my plan in faith

I will begin by writing down this semester’s deadlines and choosing study times. I will do my best, pray for help, and ask questions when I get stuck. If my plan does not work, I will change it and try again.

### Reflect on what I have learned

At the end of each month, I will write down one success, one challenge, and one next step. At the end of each semester, I will review my classes and projects. I will use what I learn to plan for the next semester.

## Refining Your Personal Goal

1. **Make a weekly note.** Every Sunday, I will write my main tasks and check off what I finish.
2. **Save what I learn.** After a project or large assignment, I will note what I did and what I want to improve. I will keep my work in one folder.
3. **Review my progress.** At the end of each month, I will check my notes and choose one change to help me move forward.

## Personal Reflection Paragraph

Writing down my goal makes it easier to see what I need to do next. Small steps help me keep going, and my notes show me how I have changed. Someday, these records may help my family understand what mattered to me and how I worked toward my goals. I can make better goals by choosing clear steps and checking my progress often. I can also pray and think about whether my choices bring me closer to God. When I write down my plans and lessons, I keep a record of my effort and the help I receive along the way.
